F4SN TV — CANCELLED

F4SN executives regret to announce that F4SN TV has been cancelled after one episode due to extreme budget constraints, questionable financial decisions and the discovery that producing television apparently requires more than YouTube and blind confidence.

The network's official investigation cited studio expenses, production costs, an alarming absence of functioning financial controls and several purchases nobody in management remembers authorizing. Ratings were described only as “information we have chosen not to make public.”

The production department has been dissolved. The studio lights have been returned. Several executives now deny approving the project at all.

When asked to explain the network's financial position, F4SN CFO NAME REDACTED responded: “Define ‘money.’”

Following an exhaustive search for a replacement medium requiring fewer cameras, microphones, animators, actors, editors, production assistants, lawyers and functioning financial controls, F4SN is proud to announce its newest division.
